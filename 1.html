Common Questions

Is this a Google Sheets template or add-on?

No. CallCare is a standalone web application that your team logs into. It connects to your Google Sheet as its database, but your CHWs use a professional, mobile-friendly interface—not the spreadsheet itself. Your managers can still access the raw sheet anytime.

Is patient data secure?

Yes. Data stays in your Google Workspace, protected by Google's enterprise security. Access is controlled via Google authentication—only users you authorize can see patient data. We never store PHI on our servers.

What does it cost after the pilot?

We offer two models: a one-time setup fee (you own the system forever) or a low monthly subscription with ongoing support. Pricing depends on team size and customization needs. We'll discuss options at the end of your pilot.

How long does setup take?

For a standard pilot, we can have your custom system ready in 3-5 business days. Complex integrations or large datasets may take up to 2 weeks. You'll receive a video walkthrough before your team starts using it.

Can you add AI features like call summaries?

Yes. Once your core system is working, we can integrate AI-powered features like automatic call note summaries, risk scoring based on conversation content, and smart follow-up recommendations. Ask us during your pilot consultation.
