Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just a Google Sheets add-on?

No. CallCare is custom software built specifically for your workflow. Your Google Sheet serves as the database, but your team gets a professional interface with features designed for call management, not spreadsheet editing.

What happens to my data?

Your data stays in your Google Sheet, under your complete control. You can export it, back it up, or analyze it however you want. CallCare reads and writes to your sheet, but never stores your data on our servers.

How is this different from a CRM?

Traditional CRMs lock your data in their system and force you to adapt to their workflow. CallCare is custom-built around your existing process and keeps your data in a format you already understand and control.

What if I need changes after delivery?

We include 30 days of adjustments with every system. After that, we're available for ongoing consultation and system improvements on an hourly basis.

How secure is this?

Security is based on Google's infrastructure and your domain access controls. Only users you authorize can access the system. No passwords to manage - access is controlled through your Google Workspace domain.

Can you integrate AI features?

Absolutely. Once your basic system is working, we can add AI-powered features like call summaries, automated follow-up suggestions, risk scoring, and intelligent routing. This is where the real magic happens.
