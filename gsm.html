Calculations & Explanations

Z-Score Calculation

Z-score = (Measured Value - Median) / (Median × SD)

Where:

  • Measured Value: The child's actual measurement
  • Median: 50th percentile for age and gender
  • SD: Standard deviation for age and gender

Interpretation Guidelines

Z-Score Range Interpretation Percentile
Above +3 Severely High >99.9th
+2 to +3 High 97.7th-99.9th
-2 to +2 Normal Range 2.3rd-97.7th
-3 to -2 Low 0.1st-2.3rd
Below -3 Severely Low <0.1st
